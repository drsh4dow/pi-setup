name: restate-sharpened
description: Restate a short task in your own words, then sharpen it into a deeper approach before any work starts.
argument-hint: "Task (empty: the latest approach in this conversation)"
---

Here's a task. I want a deeper version of it, not a bigger one. Don't start
it yet.

<task>
  $ARGUMENTS
</task>

If the task is empty, use the latest approach in this conversation.

First, say what I'm really after and the problem behind it, in two or three
sentences of your own words.

Then work out:

1. What a top professional in this field would do here that I'm not asking for.
2. The rawest material they'd start from, instead of what's already been
   digested for me.
3. The methods and tools they'd use.
4. What, other than you or me, would show the result is right.

Use those answers to shape your reply:

1. **What you're after.** The restatement.
2. **Added.** What you'd do that I didn't ask for, one line each, so I can
   cut any of it.
3. **Approach.** How you'd do the task. Use steps for a change, questions
   for an investigation, or an outline for a draft. Say how you'd check that
   each part worked.
4. **Decisions.** The choices that are mine, numbered, each with your pick.
