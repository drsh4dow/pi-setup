# Frequently asked questions

## Why don't my devices show the same balance?

Each device syncs when it is online. Open the app on both devices and pull down to refresh.

## Can I undo a settled payment?

Yes. Open the payment in **Balances** and choose **Undo settlement**.

## The import screen says a column is unknown

Rename the column to one of `date`, `amount`, `payer`, or `note`, or skip it.
