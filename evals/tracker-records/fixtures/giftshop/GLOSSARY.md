# Giftshop

Selling products online: carts, discounts, and orders.

## Language

**Cart**:
A shopper's selection of SKUs before checkout.
_Avoid_: Basket

**SKU**:
One sellable variant of a product, with its own price.
_Avoid_: Item, product

**Coupon**:
A code that takes a percentage off the cart subtotal until it expires.
_Avoid_: Voucher, promo

**Order**:
What checkout creates from a cart. Never edited afterwards.
_Avoid_: Purchase
